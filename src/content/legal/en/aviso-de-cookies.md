# Cookie notice

This notice explains which tracking technologies pagadesarrollos.com uses. It supplements the [Privacy notice](/en/privacy-notice) of PAGA Desarrollos, S.A. de C.V.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as web beacons, pixels or the browser's local storage, do much the same job. In this notice we call all of them "cookies".

Some are needed for a site to work. Others measure visits, remember preferences or show advertising, and can make it possible to identify or follow a person across sites.

## 2. What this site uses today

As of [FECHA DE ÚLTIMA ACTUALIZACIÓN]:

- **We use no analytics, advertising or social media cookies.**
- We measure visits in aggregate with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify people: it records page views without storing data that reveals who you are. [JURÍDICO TO CONFIRM this wording.]
- **We use no third-party pixels or web beacons.**
- **We load no fonts or other resources from outside services** that could record your visit: fonts are served from the site itself.
- **Browser session storage (not a cookie).** During your visit, the site keeps in your own browser the first page you opened, the site you came from and, if any, the campaign name in the link. It is not sent anywhere while you browse. Only if you submit the contact form is it included with your message, so we know how you found us. It is deleted when you close the tab. The site's hosting provider, [PROVEEDOR DE HOSPEDAJE], may log technical details of each request (such as IP address) to deliver pages and protect the service. These logs are not used to build visitor profiles.

## 3. Sites we link to

The site links to other websites, such as our projects' sales sites (for example, clubsonoma.mx and balconesresidencial.mx), maps and social networks. When you click, you leave pagadesarrollos.com. Those sites may set their own cookies and have their own privacy and cookie notices; we suggest you read them.

## 4. If we add analytics or other cookies in the future

If PAGA decides to measure site use or add third-party tools, before switching them on we will:

1. **Update this notice** and the privacy notice, with the date of the change, stating which tool is used, what data it collects, why, who the provider is and how long the data is kept.
2. **Tell you when you arrive on the site**, with a visible notice that we use these technologies and how to turn them off.
3. **Prefer tools that use no cookies and do not identify people** (for example, aggregate, cookie-free analytics). [JURÍDICO TO DECIDE: if analytics or advertising cookies that process personal data are ever used, whether to ask for prior acceptance ("Accept / Reject") before activating them.]
4. **Tell you how to turn them off**, on the site itself or in your browser.

## 5. How to control cookies in your browser

Every browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Security"). Blocking a site's strictly necessary cookies may affect how it works.
