# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data PAGA Desarrollos processes, why, and how you can exercise your rights. It is issued under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (*Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares*), published in the Official Gazette (DOF) on March 20, 2025 (the "Law"), and the rules issued under it.

## 1. Who is responsible

**PAGA Desarrollos, S.A. de C.V.** ("PAGA", "we"), with its address at [DOMICILIO FISCAL], is the controller of your personal data.

For anything related to this notice or your data, contact [RESPONSABLE / DEPARTAMENTO DE DATOS PERSONALES]:

- Email: [CORREO DE CONTACTO DE DATOS PERSONALES]
- Phone: [TELÉFONO]
- Address: [DOMICILIO PARA RECIBIR SOLICITUDES, SI ES DISTINTO DEL FISCAL]

## 2. What this website does today

As of [FECHA DE ÚLTIMA ACTUALIZACIÓN], pagadesarrollos.com is an informational website:

- It has no forms and no user accounts.
- It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies (see the [Cookie notice](/en/cookie-notice)).
- It serves its fonts from the site itself, with no calls to third-party services such as Google Fonts.

Like any web server, the hosting provider, [PROVEEDOR DE HOSPEDAJE — e.g. Vercel Inc., United States], may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. PAGA does not use these logs to identify you or for commercial purposes.

This notice also covers any contact channel PAGA adds in the future (a contact form or a contact email published on the site). If you write to us through one of them, the following applies.

## 3. What personal data we process

When you contact us, we may process:

- Your name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- The company or organization you represent, and your position, if you give it.
- The content of your message, including anything you choose to share about land, a business proposal or your interest in a project.

**We do not ask for sensitive personal data** (for example, health, ethnic origin, beliefs or preferences). Please do not include it in your message. If you send it anyway, we will not use it and will delete it.

We do not collect financial or asset data through this channel. If a later conversation requires it (for example, to formalize a purchase or a partnership), we will give you the relevant privacy notice and ask for your express consent, as the Law requires.

2. Evaluating business proposals you send us: land offers, co-investment, co-development, financing, services or supply.
3. Passing your interest in a project on to the sales team or to that project's sales website.
4. Following up on the conversation you started and keeping a record of it.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as needed to answer your request or evaluate your proposal, and then for [PLAZO DE CONSERVACIÓN] to handle follow-up questions or legal obligations. After that period, we block and then delete it, as the Law provides.

## 8. How we protect it

We maintain administrative, technical and physical security measures to protect your data against damage, loss, alteration, destruction, and unauthorized use, access or processing. Everyone involved in processing must keep it confidential, even after their relationship with PAGA ends.

If a security breach significantly affects your financial or personal rights, we will tell you immediately so that you can take action.

## 9. Your ARCO rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** know what data we hold about you and how we use it.
- **Rectification:** correct your data if it is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Cancellation:** ask us to delete it from our records, except where the Law requires us to keep it.
- **Opposition:** object to its processing, or to a specific purpose, on legitimate grounds.

Exercising one right is not a condition for exercising another, and you may exercise them at any time.

### How to submit a request

Send your request to [CORREO DE CONTACTO DE DATOS PERSONALES] or deliver it to [DOMICILIO PARA RECIBIR SOLICITUDES]. It must include:

1. Your name, and an address or email to receive our reply.
2. A document proving your identity (for example, valid official ID) or, if you act for someone else, proof of your authority to represent them.
3. A clear description of the data concerned (not needed for access requests).
4. The right you wish to exercise and what you are asking for.
5. Anything else that helps us locate your information.

For rectification, also state the change you want and attach any supporting document.

### Deadlines

- We will reply within **20 business days** of receiving your complete request.
- If the request is granted, we will carry it out within **15 business days** of our reply.
- Each period may be extended once, for the same length of time, when justified; we will let you know if so.

### How we provide the information

Access is provided by making your data available to you, as plain copies or electronic documents, [MEDIO DE ENTREGA — e.g. by email to the registered address], once your identity has been verified.

### Cost

Exercising ARCO rights is free. We may only charge justified shipping or reproduction costs. If you supply the medium, there is no charge. If you repeat the same request within twelve months, the cost will not exceed three UMA (Mexico's Unit of Measure and Update), as set out in Article 34 of the Law.

### When we may refuse a request

We may refuse a request, with reasons, in the cases set out in the Law; for example, if identity or representation is not proven, if we do not hold your data, if a third party's rights would be harmed, if a legal impediment or an authority's decision prevents it, or if the rectification, cancellation or opposition has already been carried out.

## 10. How to withdraw your consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without retroactive effect. Write to [CORREO DE CONTACTO DE DATOS PERSONALES] with your name, a way to reply to you, ID (or proof of representation), and the processing or purpose for which you are withdrawing consent.

We will reply within the same deadlines as an ARCO request. If you withdraw consent for a necessary purpose, we may be unable to continue handling your request or proposal, and in some cases the Law requires us to keep certain data.

## 11. How to limit the use or disclosure of your data

In addition to your ARCO rights, you can ask us at any time to stop sending you communications or to limit the use of your data by writing to [CORREO DE CONTACTO DE DATOS PERSONALES]. If a communication includes an unsubscribe option, you can also use it.

## 12. Changes to this notice

We may change this notice because of changes in the law, in our processes or in the website's services. The current version will be published at pagadesarrollos.com/aviso-de-privacidad, with its update date at the top. If a change affects purposes that require your consent, we will ask for it again.

## 13. Authority

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, or you disagree with our reply, you may go to the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno** (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance), Mexico's federal authority for personal data held by private parties, within the deadlines set by the Law.
