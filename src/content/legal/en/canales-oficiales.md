# Official channels and fraud prevention

Some people use the names of real estate developers to offer properties that do not exist or to ask for deposits. This notice lists the official channels of PAGA Desarrollos, S.A. de C.V. ("PAGA") and explains how to check that a message is genuine.

## 1. What PAGA never does

- **We never ask for deposits, reservation fees or down payments to accounts in an individual's name**, or to accounts of third parties unrelated to the project.
- **We never ask for payment through social media messages, WhatsApp, text messages or emails** that do not come from the channels listed in this notice.
- **We never ask for cash outside our offices, cryptocurrency, gift cards or money-transfer services.**
- **We never ask for passwords, verification codes or full card details.**
- **We never pressure you to pay "today" because an offer is about to end.**

## 2. How payments are made

Payments for any transaction are made only:

- To accounts in the name of [RAZÓN SOCIAL RECEPTORA DE PAGOS — e.g. the project company or trust], and
- Following the written instructions in the contract or in a document signed by an authorized representative.

Before paying, confirm the account by calling [TELÉFONO OFICIAL] or writing to [CORREO OFICIAL DE VERIFICACIÓN]. Do not use the phone number or email given in the message asking you to pay.

## 4. Warning signs

Be careful if:

- You are offered a price far below market, or an "exclusive discount" in exchange for paying immediately.
- Someone writes to you from a free email account (for example @gmail.com or @hotmail.com) or from a domain that looks like ours with letters changed.
- You are asked to keep the payment to yourself or to make it outside our offices.
- Bank details are changed at the last minute "because of a problem with the account".
- You are not given a contract, or are not allowed time to review it.

## 5. If you suspect fraud

1. **Do not make any payment** and do not share personal or bank details.
2. **Check with PAGA** through the channels in this notice: [CORREO OFICIAL DE VERIFICACIÓN] or [TELÉFONO OFICIAL].
3. **Keep the evidence:** screenshots, emails, phone numbers and any bank details you were given.
4. If you have already paid, **contact your bank immediately** and report it to the competent authorities. [JURÍDICO TO DECIDE: authorities to name in Mexico and in Texas.]

PAGA is not responsible for payments made to accounts or people other than those stated in a signed contract and confirmed through official channels.
